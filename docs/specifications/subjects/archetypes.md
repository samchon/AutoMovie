# Subject archetypes

## Fixed registered archetypes {#fixed-registered-archetypes}

`@automovie/archetypes` exposes deterministic builders through a host-owned registry keyed by stable IDs. Blank and duplicate identities are refused before a recipe can resolve ambiguously.
