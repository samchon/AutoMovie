# Narrative principles

These principles bind the storyline, scenario, and script ladders of a film production. They govern identity and continuity across the ladder, while each layer's own principles govern what that layer contributes.

## Unit identity {#unit-identity}

Matching H2 sequences, H3 scenes, and H4 beats preserve one stable identity from storyline through scenario to script. A refinement may add the detail owned by its layer but may not merge, split, or silently replace its parent unit.

Review question: does every unit refine exactly one same-level parent while retaining the same dramatic event?

Sources: [Plan-and-Write on hierarchical planning and realization](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v33i01.33017378); [Detailed Outline Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.10077)

## Boundary-state inheritance {#state-continuity}

Every boundary state asserted by a storyline unit remains present in its matching scenario and script unit. A lower layer may add only the physical or audiovisual detail it owns; it may not replace an inherited subject, knowledge, location, time, or terminal relation. Horizontal entry and exit completeness inside one scenario remains the scenario principle's concern.

Review question: which inherited boundary fact can be followed unchanged through the three matching units, and which added facts belong only to the refining layer?

Sources: [Zwaan, Langston, and Graesser on event-indexing dimensions](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.1995.tb00513.x); [Zacks and colleagues on perceived event boundaries](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8710938/)

## Observable inheritance {#observable-inheritance}

Every narrative layer retains the parent unit's required audience-visible change while translating it into its own decision type. It may strengthen a testable observation but may not replace an inherited requirement with an implementation convenience.

Review question: what single audience-visible change survives from the storyline through physical staging to the final screenplay?

Sources: [Detailed Outline Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.10077); [NASA systems engineering handbook on requirement traceability](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/systems-engineering-handbook/)
