# ARMY

The mass the sentinel commands. It is one subject, not many: the film never
asks the audience to follow an individual inside it.

## Silhouette

Ranks and files legible as ranks and files. The unit reads by its edges and its
intervals, so anything that destroys the interval destroys the subject.

## Scale

Members are 1.7 m, a head shorter than the sentinel, so the two subjects read
apart in one frame without a scale break.

## Cohesion

Ordered while the signal is given, and still ordered after it. Any loosening is
a dramatic event and must be authored as one, never left to chance.
