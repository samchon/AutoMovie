# Storyline Discovery

The storyline-specific outcome that the complete active storyline H2 population must search for and account for. Apply `common.md` and `films.md` in the same pass for broader contracts.

## Work-specific storyline requirements {#work-specific-storyline-requirements}

Discover every production-specific connection, progression, ordering, accumulation, formal pattern, or audience-facing development condition whose absence would make the storyline violate the adopted production contract. Derive the requirements from the film rather than a shared plot template, and give each material result an actual storyline H2 owner or a production-local principle, obligation, or evidence relationship with a typed claim.

A positive acknowledgement identifies the current authored owners and contract wiring. If the complete search finds no independent storyline result, one population-wide exclusion names the concrete connection, progression, ordering, accumulation, audience-change, and form risks examined and the shared owners that fully cover them. Naming a genre, plot shape, pace, mood, or progression style without observable conditions and actual realization does not cover this duty.

Review question: which treatment-layer relationship or development condition is specific to this film and absent from the reusable storyline contract?

Sources: [DOME on hierarchical outlines organizing long-form narrative units](https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-long.63/); [Burton and Poster on testing the function and development of authored units](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/book-you-need-to-read-to-write-the-book-you-want-to-write/beginnings-and-endings-tension-and-pace/7B84CB546C696FD1B6A8AB753522B42C)
