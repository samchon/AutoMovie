# Brief Discovery

The brief-specific outcome that the complete active brief H2 population must search for and account for. Apply `common.md` in the same pass for broader contracts.

## Work-specific brief requirements {#work-specific-brief-requirements}

Discover every production-specific delivery, shot-allocation, viewing-condition, observation, acceptance, representation, sound, silence, or transition condition needed to answer the bounded brief without importing an unnecessary film ladder. Derive the requirements from the actual promised result rather than a generic turntable, demonstration, or short-form recipe, and give each material result an actual brief H2 owner or a production-local principle, obligation, or evidence relationship with a typed claim.

A positive acknowledgement identifies the current authored owners and contract wiring. If the complete search finds no independent brief result, one population-wide exclusion names the concrete delivery, observation, falsifiability, and realization risks examined and the shared owners that fully cover them. A format label, shot count, style aspiration, or assertion that the result is simple does not cover this duty.

Review question: which bounded delivery or observation condition is specific to this production and absent from the reusable brief contract?
