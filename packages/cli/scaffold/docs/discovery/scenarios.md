# Scenario Discovery

The scenario-specific outcome that the complete active scenario H2 population must search for and account for. Apply `common.md` and `films.md` in the same pass for broader contracts.

## Work-specific scenario requirements {#work-specific-scenario-requirements}

Discover every production-specific execution condition needed to turn the adopted storyline into realizable physical or formal progression without losing its form, access, consequence, continuity, or subject integrity. Derive the requirements from the film rather than a shared scene template, and give each material result an actual scenario H2 owner or a production-local principle, obligation, or evidence relationship with a typed claim.

A positive acknowledgement identifies the current authored owners and contract wiring. If the complete search finds no independent scenario result, one population-wide exclusion names the concrete staging, continuity, exchange, timing, resource, and transition risks examined and the shared owners that fully cover them. A field label, generic scene recipe, or assertion that the outline is executable does not cover this duty.

Review question: which execution condition is specific to this film and must be fixed before the screenplay chooses final audiovisual expression?

Sources: [Zacks and colleagues on event segmentation through changes in time, cause, character, place, and goal](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8710938/); [Harvard Writing Center on transitions making relations between units explicit](https://writingcenter.fas.harvard.edu/transitions)
