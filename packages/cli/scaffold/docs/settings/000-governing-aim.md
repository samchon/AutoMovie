The configured production id is this starter's repository identity and temporary working title. Its logline is: “A soloist raises a hand, and a chorus standing in rows answers it.” The deterministic blocking pass realizes that answer as a 2 m chorus advance whose held result remains readable. Its authored ladder ends in two six-second screenplay sources whose half-second dissolve produces an 11.5-second final timeline. Required output consists of a deterministic preview, a deterministic feature, one pose guide pass, English accessibility captions, and a zero-gain audio mix whose only source is an inert structural pipeline fixture. It is deliberately not a direct brief or a reusable-only library; a production whose complete intent fits one non-narrative observation changes `kind` to `brief`, while one that delivers no shots changes it to `library`.

All distances are metres, all durations are seconds, the world is right-handed with positive Y up, and `plaza-center` is the origin on the ground. These are chosen repository conventions, not measurements of an external place.
