# SOLOIST

The one figure the camera can follow. Stands ahead of the rows so the raised
hand reads against open ground rather than against other bodies.

## Silhouette

Upright, single, and separated from the group. Readable at the delivery raster
from the widest shot the film uses; if the silhouette stops reading there, the
scene has lost its subject.

## Scale

1.8 m, taller than the rows by design, so the figure ahead of the group reads
as the one to follow before the hand moves.

## Capability

Raises and holds one hand. Nothing else is claimed, because nothing else is
dramatized: a capability this document does not state is one the source must
not assume.
