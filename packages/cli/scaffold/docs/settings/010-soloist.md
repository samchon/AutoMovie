# SOLOIST

## Identity and scale {#soloist-identity-scale}

SOLOIST is the one figure the audience follows. It is upright, single, and separated from the group, with a chosen occupied height of 1.8 m. Its whole-body silhouette and raised hand must remain distinguishable at the delivery review condition declared in the art-direction settings; if either disappears there, the production has lost its subject.

## Hand capability {#soloist-hand-capability}

SOLOIST exposes one articulated state: either hand down or one hand raised and held. The shoulder remains attached, the body remains planted at its assigned position, and no walking, facial performance, or second-arm action is authorized by this starter.
