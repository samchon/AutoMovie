# Art direction

## Delivery review condition {#art-delivery-review-condition}

Review the blocking pass at a chosen 1280 by 720, 16:9 delivery raster, 24 frames per second, and sRGB output color space, and at the widest camera view in which each subject is used. A required silhouette, formation interval, raised-hand state, or 2 m group displacement that cannot be distinguished there fails even if it becomes visible in a closer diagnostic view. Unequal-scale facts need not be forced into one false master image: a cut or dissolve may distribute them, but each subject must remain distinguishable in the delivered view and review frame that owns its claim.

## Palette and scale grammar {#art-palette-scale}

Use one primitive-3D blocking style and exactly five production swatches: dark background `#182235`, saturated SOLOIST accent `#d7b56d`, desaturated CHORUS body `#8f9d74`, desaturated GATE finish `#6f746e`, and neutral haze `#89918a`. Neutral ground introduces no authored material swatch. A standing 1.8 m figure is the shared scale reference; represented extents are stated in metres against that figure rather than against pixel count.

## Effects and audio boundary {#art-effects-audio-absence}

One deterministic low-contrast haze effect may occupy the declared far region of PLAZA without competing with subject silhouettes. No other visible effect and no audible content is authored; the zero-gain structural audio guide exists only to prove the delivery pipeline, and silence is a scope fact rather than a completed creative choice a later production must preserve.
