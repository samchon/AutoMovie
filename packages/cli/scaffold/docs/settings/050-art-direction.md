# Art direction

The constraints every subject in this production is drawn against. They are
settings facts, not a schedule: nothing here says which scene uses what.

## Palette {#palette}

Neutral ground, one saturated accent reserved for the subject the film is
following. Chosen rather than measured. The accent exists so a silhouette can be
found in one frame without a caption, and spending it on a second subject spends
the only thing that makes the first one findable.

## Scale grammar {#scale-grammar}

A standing figure is the unit. Every authored extent states itself in metres and
is sized against that figure rather than against the frame, so a subject that
reads at one raster still reads at another.

## Review distance {#review-distance}

Subjects are judged at the distance the film actually shows them from, which for
this production is the widest shot each subject appears in. A detail that
resolves only closer than that is cost without effect.

Chosen from the shot list rather than measured from geometry, and it moves when
the shot list moves.

## Effects and audio {#effects-and-audio}

Neither is authored in this sample. Recorded here as an explicit absence so a
later production does not read the silence as a decision already made.
