# SCN-001 — The cue

EXT. PLAZA — DAY

On the plaza, the SOLOIST steps ahead of the CHORUS and raises one hand. A
MARKER POST stands at the point stepped to, low enough to keep clear of the
raised hand. The rows remain in order behind the gesture long enough for the cue
to read.
