# SEQ-CUE — The call

A soloist raises a hand while the chorus behind stays visibly in its rows.

The sequence exists to make the gesture legible as a cue rather than as an
isolated pose, so the rows have to read as rows while it happens — and they are
moving while it happens, closing on the figure as one without breaking their
order. What it leaves behind is a group that has come nearer and has not yet
been answered, which is the only reason the sequence after it has anything to
do.
