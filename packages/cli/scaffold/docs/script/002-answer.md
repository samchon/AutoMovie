# SCN-002 — The answer

EXT. PLAZA — DAY, CONTINUOUS

The SOLOIST holds the plaza, then gives the answering motion. The CHORUS
answers as one, closing two metres together, and remains the visible
consequence of that exchange rather than anonymous background.

At the far edge of the ground the GATE stands shut on its one hinge. It is the
only thing here that could change and nothing in this film changes it, so it
reads as the stake the answer lands near rather than as the answer itself.

The soloist holds the raised cue from the first frame until the answering
motion lands, which the shot contract places between 3.0 and 5.0 seconds of a
6.0 second scene. The hold before the answer is therefore **at least 3.0
seconds**.
