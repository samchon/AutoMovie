# SCN-001 — The cue

EXT. PLAZA — DAY

On the plaza, the SOLOIST stands ahead of the CHORUS and raises one hand. The
rows close two metres on the figure as the hand goes up, holding their order the
whole way.

The hand reaches its height inside the first half of the scene and stays there
until it ends. Read from the shot contract: the cue window closes at 3.0
seconds and the scene runs 6.0 seconds, so the hold is **at least 3.0 seconds**.
That hold is the cue; a shorter one reads as a gesture that failed to land.
