# SCN-001 — The cue

EXT. PLAZA — DAY

On the plaza, the SOLOIST stands ahead of the CHORUS and raises one hand. The
rows remain in order behind the gesture, and nothing else on the ground moves.

The hand reaches its height inside the first half of the scene and stays there
until it ends. Read from the shot contract: the cue window closes at 3.0
seconds and the scene runs 6.0, so the hold is **at least 3.0 seconds**. That
hold is the cue; a shorter one reads as a gesture that failed to land.
