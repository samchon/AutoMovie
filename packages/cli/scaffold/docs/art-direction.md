# Art direction

The sample proves a primitive silhouette, beauty pass, mask pass, and pose
pass. Record palette, scale grammar, camera readability, LOD, effect, and audio
decisions before expanding the production.

Say of each decision whether the film chose it or a source fixed it. A decision
a source fixes cites the record in `docs/research` that fixes it; a decision the
film chose is the film's, and saying so is what keeps a departure from reading
as an error.
