# Cue — the hand goes up

**Entry state.** The chorus stands in its rows on the plaza. The soloist stands
among them, indistinguishable in silhouette from the group.

**Action.** The soloist steps forward to `plaza-center`, clear of the front row,
and raises one hand. The chorus holds its rows behind the gesture.

**Exit state.** The soloist stands clear of the rows with the hand up. The
chorus has been addressed and has not moved.

The hold after the hand reaches its height is what makes this a cue rather than
a pose; the script fixes how long it is.
