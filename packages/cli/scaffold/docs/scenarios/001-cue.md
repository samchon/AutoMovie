# Cue — the hand goes up

**Entry state.** The chorus stands in its rows on the plaza. The soloist stands
on `plaza-center`, clear of the front row, with the hand down.

**Action.** The soloist raises one hand and holds it there. Across the same
span the chorus closes the 2 m its own document states, moving as one and
keeping its rows.

**Exit state.** The soloist stands where it began, with the hand up. The chorus
stands two metres nearer, still in order, and has not yet been answered.

The separation the cue needs is a placement rather than an approach: the soloist
is already clear of the rows when the scene opens and stays where it stands.
Staging the figure's separation as a walk would put a second free-moving subject
in the frame, and the group's advance is already the second thing moving in it.

The hold after the hand reaches its height is what makes this a cue rather than
a pose; the script fixes how long it is.
