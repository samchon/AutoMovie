# Answer — the answering motion

**Entry state.** The soloist stands clear of the rows with the hand up. The
chorus is in order behind. The gate at the plaza edge is shut.

**Action.** The soloist holds, then gives the answering motion. The chorus
answers as one. The gate's leaf swings off its frame.

**Exit state.** The gate is open. The chorus has moved and is visibly the
consequence of the exchange.

The hold before the answer is the beat the whole film turns on. It is stated as
a duration in the script, not here, because here it is only known to be a hold.
