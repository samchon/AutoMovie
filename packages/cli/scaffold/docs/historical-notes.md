# Historical notes

For historical work, record source URL, source character, access date,
confirmed claim, disagreement, and uncertainty. Separate invented characters,
dialogue, compression, and symbolism from attested events.

## Starter line-battle values

The scaffold's musket profile is a reproducible example, not a universal
historical constant. Its 20-second reload cycle follows the Royal Armouries'
three-rounds-per-minute India Pattern benchmark. Its 100 m and 200 m accuracy
points retain the reported French tests (52/720 and 18/720 shots against the
declared target geometry), and its 305 m/s muzzle velocity and 5% scenario
misfire rate remain explicit assumptions to replace when weapon, weather,
training, or supply differs.

Sources: Royal Armouries, “Waterloo 1815”; _The Manual and Platoon Exercises_
(1804). Record the exact edition/URL and access date when revising these values.
