# Research principles

Rules a research ledger answers. A ledger records what the world outside this
production contains: what a thing looked like, how large it was, how it worked,
and who says so.

It sits beside the ladder rather than on it. Nothing above it commissions it and
nothing below it is required to use it, because a production may be entirely
invented and owe the world nothing. What binds is the other direction: a ledger
that exists is held to these rules, because a source recorded carelessly is
worse than no source at all — it carries the authority of a citation and none of
the substance.

A production that sources nothing owes nothing here.

## The source is identifiable {#source-identified}

Each entry names its source precisely enough that a second reader reaches the
same thing: a URL, or a title with its edition and page, or an institution with
an accession number. "A book about temples" is not a source; it is a memory of
one.

Sources: `ASSET_SOURCING` on provenance a distributable file must carry.

## The confidence is stated {#confidence-stated}

Say what kind of evidence this is and how much weight it carries. A photograph
of the surviving thing, a measured survey drawing, a museum reconstruction, and
an artist's impression are four different claims about reality, and a ledger
that records them identically has thrown away the only thing that separates
them.

Where the entry is an inference rather than a reading, say that it is one.

## It names what it grounds {#names-what-it-grounds}

Each entry says which settings fact it stands behind. A source that grounds
nothing is either unused work or a fact somebody forgot to write down, and both
are worth finding.

The naming is prose rather than a citation, and deliberately so: research does
not oblige canon. A production is free to invent every figure it uses, so canon
is never asked to prove that somebody researched it.

## A disagreement is recorded, not resolved silently {#contradiction-kept}

Where sources conflict, keep the conflict. Write both readings, say which one
the production took, and say that taking it was a choice. A ledger that records
only the winning reading looks like agreement, and the next author has no way to
know a decision was ever made.

Sources: `principles/common.md#stated-basis` on chosen figures saying so.

## It is not a settings document {#not-a-settings-document}

Research reports what the world contains. It never fixes what this production
contains. The figure this production is held to lives in `docs/settings`, and a
number that appears only in a ledger is a number nothing implements and nothing
answers for.

Where a ledger changes what the production should be, change the settings
document; the ledger is why, not what.

Sources: `principles/common.md#change-upstream-first`; `EVIDENCE_GRAPH` on
which rung owns which decision.
