# Space and circulation

Rules a place answers. A place is not a backdrop: it is a set of spaces, the
boundaries between them, and the routes that connect them, and a production
that leaves any of the three unstated has left the staging to guesswork.

A production with no built environment owes nothing here.

## A space states its extent and what encloses it {#space-and-enclosure}

Every space names the volume it occupies and the surfaces that bound it, and
says which of those surfaces it owns. A slab between two storeys belongs to
neither room alone, and a boundary nobody owns is counted by every gate and
maintained by none.

Sources: `BUILT_ENVIRONMENT` on boundary records, spaces and realizing
elements; `principles/authoring/settings.md#surface-ownership`.

## Circulation is stated at three levels {#circulation-levels}

Say how the place is moved through: the primary route that carries the main
movement, the secondary routes inside a space, and the adjacencies that let one
zone serve another. A plan that names only doorways has described openings, not
circulation, and a scenario staged on it will ask a figure to arrive somewhere
nothing connects to.

## What is reachable is a fact, not an impression {#reachability-is-measured}

Before staging a movement, ask the model whether the destination is reachable
from the origin and by which connector. An extent, a placement, and a missing
member are numbers in an inspection and only impressions in a frame.

Sources: `BUILT_ENVIRONMENT` on connectors, adjacency and reachability;
`SUBJECT_INSPECTION`.

## Emphasis is authored, not hoped for {#hierarchy-is-authored}

Where one space or element must read as more important, say which tool does it
— size, position, material, contrast, or light — and state it as a decision. A
hierarchy nobody authored is a hierarchy the renderer chose, and it will choose
differently when anything moves.

## An enclosure declares its own section {#declare-the-section}

Where the subject to be judged is enclosed, the declaration says how the near
side is removed. Judging an enclosure from outside reports on the wall that
hides the question, and a cutaway is an inspection control rather than a
delivered frame.

Sources: `SUBJECT_INSPECTION`.
