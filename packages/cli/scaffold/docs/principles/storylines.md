# Storyline principles

Rules a storyline answers. A storyline is the narrative treatment: what happens,
why it happens, and what changes because of it. It is where the settings catalog
is first accounted for, and it is the last layer that is allowed to be about
meaning rather than about execution.

A production that authors no story owes nothing here.

## Every sequence carries a figure through an event {#figure-in-event}

A storyline unit names who or what the event happens to and what it does about
it. A sequence in which nothing is chosen is a description of a situation, not a
sequence, and no scenario written from it can be staged.

Sources: `SCREENPLAY_WRITING` on what a treatment sequence owes.

## An action has a consequence the next unit inherits {#action-consequence}

What happens changes the state the following unit begins from. A unit whose
outcome the next one does not carry is a unit the story can lose without
noticing, which is exactly what the ladder exists to expose.

Sources: `EVIDENCE_GRAPH` on abandonment being the thing the ladder catches.

## Account for the settings this unit uses, and only those {#settings-accounted}

A storyline unit cites the settings it actually uses. The settings catalog does
not prove itself, and this is the layer that decides which facts the work is
made of. A unit that cites a setting it does not use has made the citation
worthless for every reader after it.

Sources: `EVIDENCE_GRAPH` on the settings library; this graph's storyline claim
against settings.

## Name what the audience learns and when {#disclosure}

Where the work withholds something, the storyline says what is withheld, from
whom, and what ends the withholding. A reveal nobody planned is a reveal the
scenario cannot stage and the script cannot time.

Sources: `SCREENPLAY_WRITING` on treatment obligations.

## A storyline is a treatment, not a script {#treatment-not-script}

It says what happens and why. It does not fix camera, staging, blocking, or
line-level dialogue — those belong to the scenario and the script, and deciding
them here removes the decision from the layer that can test it against geometry.
