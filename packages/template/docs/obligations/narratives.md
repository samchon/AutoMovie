# Narrative obligations

Duties each complete treatment, script, and screenplay H2 owner population satisfies in its own form. Each item appears one or more times across that population, as its wording requires. These duties judge the shape of the addressed population rather than one unit in isolation and permit no exclusion.

## Unit addressability {#unit-addressability}

Treatments address their narrative axis as one flat ordered sequence of numbered event files, each with one H1 event title and anchored H2 logical event units. They have no delivery-group directory, index, H3 scene, or H4 beat. Scripts independently address the audience-facing delivery axis as numbered group directories and unit files with H1 titles and anchored H2/H3/H4 units; screenplays preserve that script address structure exactly.

Every independent narrative event has its own treatment H2. Every independent delivery sequence, scene, and beat has its own script and screenplay H2, H3, or H4. One minimum unit begins from one entry condition, carries one central dramatic or formal progression, and leaves one identifiable exit condition. Split a unit when it contains another progression with its own objective, causal or formal operation, and exit, or when one portion can move or disappear while the remainder still functions independently. Script and screenplay H2 and H3 units organize their actual descendants instead of concealing additional scenes or beats in direct prose.

A change of time, place, participant, focus, or expressive form is evidence of a possible boundary, not an automatic split. Montage, parallel action, documents, and other non-continuous forms still receive the smallest stable address that owns one coherent operation and change.

Review question: does the population hide any independently movable progression, or does any split fragment one progression without giving both parts a function and exit?

This obligation owns semantic boundaries within each narrative axis. The narrative unit-identity principle owns many-to-many treatment realization and exact script-to-screenplay lineage after those boundaries have been chosen, and the common substantive-completion principle owns authored depth inside each addressed unit.
