# Storyline obligations

These required roles must be covered across the storyline H2 sequence population and permit no exclusion. A film without deliberate withholding still owns an explicit audience route that says what is available from the start and how attention moves through it.

## Opening condition {#opening-condition}

The storylines establish the initial dramatic, physical, and audience-knowledge state from which the first consequential turn can begin.

Review question: what is stable at the opening that the film is promising to change?

Sources: [Burton and Poster on beginnings and endings](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/book-you-need-to-read-to-write-the-book-you-want-to-write/beginnings-and-endings-tension-and-pace/7B84CB546C696FD1B6A8AB753522B42C)

## Terminal condition {#terminal-condition}

The storylines establish the final changed dramatic, physical, and audience-knowledge state and show how it answers the governing aim rather than merely stopping action.

Review question: which opening uncertainty or imbalance has a different status at the final image?

Sources: [Burton and Poster on beginnings and endings](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/book-you-need-to-read-to-write-the-book-you-want-to-write/beginnings-and-endings-tension-and-pace/7B84CB546C696FD1B6A8AB753522B42C)

## Audience route {#audience-route}

The storylines allocate the production-wide order in which the audience receives, withholds, and reinterprets the information necessary to experience the promised change. This H2 owns sequencing across units; `principles/storylines.md#audience-change` separately owns the information delta inside each unit.

Review question: what must the audience know before each major turn, and what must remain unresolved until after it?

Sources: [Brewer and Lichtenstein on story structure](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0140525X00017568)

## Resolution and aftermath {#resolution-aftermath}

Closing units decide which accumulated questions, choices, relations, and images close, transform, or remain open. The result is understandable from prior conditions and leaves the audience with an observable changed or deliberately unchanged state. An open ending still supplies a reason to stop and material on which to judge it.

Review question: which accumulated promises does the ending settle or deliberately preserve, and what aftermath makes the stopping point meaningful?

This obligation owns how the ending handles prior accumulation. The terminal-condition obligation owns which H2 is the production's actual endpoint.

Sources: [Burton and Poster on the range from open to closed endings](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/prompts-you-need-to-help-you-write-the-book-you-want-to-write/beginnings-and-endings/B192F95BCB024C6EBB7F2A5C918EB5FC); [Purdue OWL on plot, climax, resolution, and theme](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/subject_specific_writing/writing_in_literature/writing_in_literature_detailed_discussion/reading_a_novel_or_story.html)

## Thematic development {#thematic-development}

Across the complete storyline population, the production's central concerns emerge through the changing use of events, choices, relations, places, images, sounds, and form. Repeated elements acquire a new context or effect instead of repeating one message, and an external explanation does not announce the intended conclusion in place of the film.

Review question: how does each recurrence across the population preserve or alter the production's central concern through an authored difference?

Sources: [UNC Writing Center on meaning emerging from fictional form and detail](https://writingcenter.unc.edu/tips-and-tools/literature-fiction/); [Magliano and colleagues on character-goal tracking and theme understanding](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4266429/)

## Treatment boundary {#treatment-boundary}

Across the complete storyline population, settle every essential narrative decision needed to produce matching production-capable scenarios. A scenario may determine spatial execution, second-by-second timing, complete dialogue, and physical blocking, but must not have to invent essential participants or forces, stakes or organizing pressure, order, alternatives where applicable, consequence, or audience effect. Storylines do not choose camera grammar or source construction.

Review question: could a scenario author stage the complete treatment without inventing essential narrative development, and which treatment sentence already chooses a lower-layer presentation decision?

This obligation owns the handoff boundary of the detailed treatment population. The common layer-boundary obligation owns file routing, and scenario and screenplay principles own their respective refinements.

Sources: [Plan-and-Write on separating planning from realization](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v33i01.33017378); [Detailed Outline Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.10077)
