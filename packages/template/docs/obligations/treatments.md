# Treatment obligations

This film-only obligation is covered across the complete treatment H2 owner population and permits no exclusion. It asks what the interval between opening and ending accumulates, a role no one unit can satisfy for the layer.

## Sustained middle {#sustained-middle}

Across the complete treatment population, keep the film's central pressure deferred between the opening and the ending instead of discharging it early or leaving it idle. Each stretch of the middle raises the cost of the outcome, narrows what remains possible, or unsettles what the audience took as settled, so reaching the ending later means reaching a different ending.

Detour, subplot, digression, and return are ways a middle does this, not evidence that it has stalled. What fails is a middle whose stretches leave the central pressure in the state in which they found it. In a long or serial film the middle is most of the work, so a run of units that could be removed without changing the ending is the finding rather than filler.

Review question: across the complete treatment population, which middle stretches raise the outcome's cost, narrow the remaining possibilities, or unsettle what the audience took as settled, and which stretch exits unchanged?

This item owns accumulation across the span between beginning and ending. `principles/narratives.md#audience-investment` owns whether one unit gives a reason to continue, `principles/narratives.md#unit-connection` owns how two units relate, and `obligations/storylines.md#resolution-aftermath` owns what the ending does with what accumulated.
