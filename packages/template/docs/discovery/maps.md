# Map Discovery

The map-specific outcome that the complete active map H2 population must search for and account for. Apply `common.md` and `designs.md` in the same pass for broader production contracts.

## Work-specific map requirements {#work-specific-map-requirements}

Discover every production-specific world condition required by the actual extent, terrain, water, ecology, land use, settlement, transport, infrastructure, weather, time state, and planned consumers. Coordinate reference, datum, source accuracy, terrain and water contact, network continuity, site access, seasonal or event state, population density, level of detail, and map delivery are possible search directions rather than a checklist. Derive the requirements from the declared world and site, source records, operative subjects and equipment, active space and instance branches, systems, and promised observations rather than a generic geography or asset catalogue. Give each material result an actual map H2 owner or a production-local principle, obligation, or evidence relationship with a typed claim.

A positive acknowledgement identifies the current map owners, decision basis, affected branch wiring, and observable realization. If the complete search finds no independent map result, one population-wide exclusion names the concrete extent, coordinate, terrain, water, ecology, network, land-use, temporal-state, source-quality, site-interface, and observation risks examined and the shared owners that fully cover them. A place name, background image, imported terrain, road label, or nominal coordinate without its adopted authority, resolved world relation, and falsifying observation does not cover this duty.

Review question: which world or site condition is specific to this production and absent from the reusable map contract?

Sources: [OGC Topic 2 on spatial referencing by coordinates](https://docs.ogc.org/as/18-005r5/18-005r5.html); [OGC CityGML 3.0 on coherent semantic and spatial urban models](https://docs.ogc.org/is/21-006r2/21-006r2.html); [NASA on eliciting requirements from stakeholder needs and system context](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/4-1-stakeholder-expectations-definition/)
