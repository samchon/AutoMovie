# Settings Discovery

The settings-specific outcomes that the complete settings H2 population must search for and account for. Apply `common.md` in the same pass for broader production contracts.

## Directive, promise, and subject requirements {#directive-promise-subject-requirements}

Discover production-specific facts, constraints, entities, dependencies, uncertainties, and boundaries required by direct directives, the delivery promise and form, existing canon and assets, subject or medium knowledge, source conflicts, and representation risks. Give each result an H2 owner or a truthful inherited-default, outside-scope, or unresolved classification. Search openly; do not fill a predetermined domain list.

A positive acknowledgement names the current owners or classifications. If none lies beyond shared coverage, one population-wide exclusion names the concrete inputs and risks examined and the shared owners that suffice. A future owner, empty file family, or audit assertion is not a result.

Review question: which direct input, promised observation, operative subject, dependency, or failure risk still lacks a settings owner or explicit classification?

Sources: [Ryan on storyworlds as reconstructed systems rather than closed inventories](https://marilaur.info/degrees.pdf); [NASA on eliciting requirements from actual stakeholder and system context](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/4-1-stakeholder-expectations-definition/)

## Planned delivery backcast {#planned-delivery-backcast}

Backcast the production's actual planned consumers to discover every production-wide fact or constraint they would otherwise have to invent. For a film, inspect planned treatments, scripts, screenplays, design branches, shots, production source, and edit. For a brief, inspect the bounded observations, design branches, shots, production source, and edit. For a library, inspect the selected design and source branches and any production serialization. Put consequential canon in settings before downstream use; route narrative order, design decisions, shot realization, and edit choices to their own owners.

A positive acknowledgement identifies the settings owners and any separately activated production contract. If the planned consumers need no additional owner, one population-wide exclusion names the consumers examined and the existing owners that make invention unnecessary.

Review question: which planned downstream consumer would still have to invent a production-wide fact, permission, constraint, identity, or review condition?

Sources: [Plan-and-Write on separating planning from realization](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v33i01.33017378); [NASA systems engineering handbook on allocation and bidirectional traceability](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/systems-engineering-handbook/)
