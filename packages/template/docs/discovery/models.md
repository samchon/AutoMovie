# Model Discovery

The model-specific outcome that the complete active model H2 population must search for and account for. Apply `common.md` and `designs.md` in the same pass for broader production contracts.

## Work-specific model requirements {#work-specific-model-requirements}

Discover every production-specific deterministic representation condition required by the actual represented subjects and their planned consumers. Subject-specific silhouette, articulation, part hierarchy, surface partition, occupied extent, precision or fit, reusable interface, fidelity limit, and neutral observation are possible search directions rather than a checklist. Derive the requirements from the governed subject, settings, source assets, downstream motion or placement, and promised use rather than a generic modeling recipe, and give each material result an actual model H2 owner or a production-local principle, obligation, or evidence relationship with a typed claim.

A positive acknowledgement identifies the current authored owners and contract wiring. If the complete search finds no independent model result, one population-wide exclusion names the concrete silhouette, articulation, partition, fit, interface, fidelity, and review risks examined and the shared owners that fully cover them. Naming a mesh type, polygon budget, rig style, or intended fidelity without its subject-specific applicability and observable realization does not cover this duty.

Review question: which representation condition is specific to these subjects and absent from the reusable model contract?

Sources: [glTF 2.0 on mesh primitives, material assignment, skins, joints, and morph targets](https://registry.khronos.org/glTF/specs/2.0/glTF-2.0.html); [OpenUSD on models, assets, prims, and transforms](https://openusd.org/release/glossary.html)
