# Film Discovery

The outcomes for production-specific conditions shared by treatments, scripts, and screenplays. Settings anticipates them during backcast, and each film prose layer rechecks them. Work-wide conditions belong in `common.md`, and conditions unique to one film prose layer belong in that layer's discovery file.

## Work-specific film requirements {#work-specific-film-requirements}

Discover every production-specific condition that must survive across treatment design, executable staging, and final audiovisual expression. Form, progression, audience access, visual or textual language, representation, sound, silence, and recurring devices are possible search directions rather than a checklist. Put each independent result in the production target matching its evidence behavior and configure stage-aligned claims over all three prose layers. During settings backcast, establish the contract and its settings prerequisites without inventing film content; each prose layer realizes it in current authored owners.

A retained contract records its production target, three-layer claim wiring, and settings prerequisites. Each film prose acknowledgement also identifies the current authored realization. If the complete search finds no independent cross-layer film result, one population-wide exclusion names the concrete formal, access, representation, and audiovisual risks examined and the shared owners that fully cover them. A genre name, style label, formal aspiration, or rule active on only one intended layer does not cover this duty.

Review question: which production-specific condition must remain recognizable and testable across all three film prose layers?
