# Direct brief principles

Briefs govern bounded deliveries such as a short-form action, simple demonstration, turntable, pose study, motion test, or visualization whose complete intent fits one delivery/shot/observation hierarchy. They are not a compressed substitute for a film that needs independent narrative refinement.

## No narrative smuggling {#no-narrative-smuggling}

Every brief H2, H3, and H4 keeps its local action inside the bounded observation contract and excludes independently authored causal character change, audience revelation, or scene-to-scene dramatic inheritance. Once the result requires any of those as a separate decision layer, the production migrates to the film kind and authors the full ladder.

Review question: would a viewer need to infer why a character chose an action or what a revelation means for the brief to succeed?

Sources: [Plan-and-Write on an explicit planning layer](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v33i01.33017378); [Zacks and colleagues on event segmentation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8710938/)
