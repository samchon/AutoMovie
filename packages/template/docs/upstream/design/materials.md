# Material upstream revision

What binding construction and appearance to reviewed surfaces proves wrong in settings, models, or spaces, and what is repaired there. Every selected material H2 answers for its actual parents.

## Parent revision from material work {#parent-revision-from-material-work}

Material work tests whether parent surfaces expose stable identities, coordinates, physical roles, conditions, and state ranges that a finish can realize. Report the exact defect it exposes: an unnamed face, incompatible texture scale, missing thickness or layering authority, an impossible response range, or a model or space boundary that changes under the required material state. Repair the owning settings, model, or space unit before rebinding; the material may not redefine geometry to make itself fit.

`@evidence` names every upstream target repaired and the binding or response test that exposed it. When this unit exposes no defect, `@evidenceExclude` names the concrete surfaces, coordinate bases, construction roles, environments, and states examined and why they were sufficient. A parentless unit or generic compatibility claim covers nothing.

Review question: what did binding this material unit prove wrong in its actual settings, model, or space parents, or which concrete parent decisions did it test and find sufficient?

Sources: [MaterialX on portable material interfaces and geometric inputs](https://materialx.org/assets/MaterialX.v1.38.Spec.pdf); [NASA on interface verification and corrective action at the source](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/systems-engineering-handbook/)
