# Film-source upstream revision

What assembling reviewed shots, production configuration, and authored tracks proves wrong in their owners, and what is repaired there. Every selected film-source export answers for its actual parents.

## Parent revision from film-source work {#parent-revision-from-film-source-work}

Assembly tests whether intervals, transitions, source-time mappings, captions, audio, effects, and delivery endpoints form one deterministic timeline without new creative decisions. Report any gap, overlap, incompatible duration, missing auxiliary content, ambiguous selection, or publication value the export could not assemble as authored. Repair the screenplay or brief, shot, settings, design, source, or production owner that owns the defect; editorial code may not invent replacement content or hide a missing interval.

`@evidence` names every upstream target repaired and the assembly or publication case that exposed it. When the export exposes no defect, `@evidenceExclude` names the concrete shot intervals, track mappings, transition boundaries, configuration, and outputs assembled as written. A parentless export or generic successful-build claim covers nothing.

Review question: what did assembling this film-source export prove wrong in its actual parents, or which concrete parent decisions did it test and find sufficient?

Sources: [SMPTE ST 2067-3 on explicit composition playlists and track resources](https://pub.smpte.org/doc/st2067-3/20220511-pub/); [W3C PROV-O on derivation and invalidation of generated entities](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/)
