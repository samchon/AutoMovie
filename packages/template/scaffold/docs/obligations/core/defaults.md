# Post-draft anti-default obligations

These questions become meaningful only after one selected authored population is complete. Counts locate literal passages and never decide merit, provenance, or authorship.

## Recurrent frame distribution {#recurrent-frame-distribution}

Group repeated contrasts, qualifications, enumerations, transitions, predicates, and closures by their literal operation. Different material may share a frame when refrain, ritual, institutional form, accessibility convention, or another declared constraint owns it; recurrence with no shared material function is repaired at the smallest responsible owners.

Review question: which frame recurs across unlike material, which complete membership establishes the pattern, and what production-owned function protects any retained use?

## Surface cadence distribution {#surface-cadence-distribution}

Map sentence length, paragraph movement, breaks, emphasis, and handoff patterns to the pressures and forms they carry. Deliberately even procedure and formal repetition pass; unrelated operations converging on one cadence without cause do not. Repair the material boundary rather than injecting random variation.

Review question: which cadence dominates materially different units, and does the production own the reason for that regularity?

Sources: [Padmakumar and He on measured diversity loss in model-assisted writing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.05196); [Purdue OWL on repeated sentence patterns](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/academic_writing/sentence_variety/similar_sentence_patterns_or_rhythms.html)
