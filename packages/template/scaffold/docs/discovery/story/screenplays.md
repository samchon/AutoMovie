# Screenplay Discovery

The screenplay-specific outcome that the complete active screenplay H2 population must search for and account for. Apply `common.md` and `films.md` in the same pass for broader contracts.

## Work-specific screenplay requirements {#work-specific-screenplay-requirements}

Discover every production-specific visible, written, audible, silent, timing, access, voice, rhythm, or expressive condition needed to realize the adopted audience experience without moving shot or edit authorship into the screenplay. These are possible manifestations rather than a required inventory. Derive the requirements from the film, make labels such as a named style or tone observable, and give each material result an actual screenplay H2 owner or a production-local principle, obligation, or evidence relationship with a typed claim.

A positive acknowledgement identifies the current authored owners and contract wiring. If the complete search finds no independent screenplay result, one population-wide exclusion names the concrete audiovisual expression and audience-access risks examined and the shared owners that fully cover them. Naming a style, influence, atmosphere, or intended emotion without observable choices and actual realization does not cover this duty.

Review question: which final audience-facing expressive condition is specific to this film and not already owned by the reusable screenplay contract?

Sources: [BBC screenplay format and examples](https://downloads.bbc.co.uk/writersroom/scripts/screenplay.pdf); [Bordwell on films as patterned systems that guide spectator construction](https://www.routledge.com/Narration-in-the-Fiction-Film/Bordwell/p/book/9780415018777); [Chion on sound and image forming one audiovisual mode of perception](https://cup.columbia.edu/book/audio-vision-sound-on-screen/9780231185882/)
