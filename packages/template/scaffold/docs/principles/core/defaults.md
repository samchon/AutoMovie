# Composition-safe anti-default principles

Language-neutral defects one authored H2, H3, or H4 can establish from its own body. Population recurrence belongs to `obligations/core/defaults.md`; target-language grammar, idiom, and register belong to the selected `docs/language` module. No vocabulary, punctuation, construction, or unit count is banned by itself.

## Purposeful enumeration {#purposeful-enumeration}

Every enumerated member changes the set by contributing a distinct class, operation, image, input, or consequence. The number of members is not the finding; a member removable without loss was added only to complete a habitual shape.

Review question: which member can disappear without losing a distinction or operation, and what material difference should own its place?

## Earned significance {#earned-significance}

A modifier that claims consequence or interpretation names an observable action, relation, result, or constraint established by the material. It cannot manufacture importance that the unit has not earned.

Review question: which significance-claiming modifier can disappear without losing an event, relation, observation, or decision?

## Responsive qualification {#responsive-qualification}

A qualification preserves a real uncertainty, exception, viewpoint, tolerance, or competing claim. A balance against an objection that neither the material nor the reader situation raises is decorative.

Review question: which qualification changes nothing an intended reader may infer, and which actual boundary should replace it?

## Functional formatting {#functional-formatting}

Typography and punctuation mark a specific interruption, hierarchy, embedded document, accessibility state, or formal turn. A chosen system and a necessary technical notation pass; a mark that can disappear without loss does not.

Review question: which formatting mark names no distinction owned by this unit or the production's declared system?

## Material contrast {#contrastive-definition}

Both terms of a contrast are live possibilities in this host, and removing either loses meaning. A negative term introduced only to frame an intact positive claim is not a material contrast.

Review question: which asserted opposition disappears without changing the positive claim because the competing possibility never existed here?
