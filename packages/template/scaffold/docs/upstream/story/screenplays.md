# Screenplay upstream revision

What final audiovisual realization proves wrong in scripts, treatments, or settings, and what is repaired there. Every selected screenplay H2, H3, and H4 answers for its actual script parent and any earlier owner implicated by the finding.

## Script and canon revision from screenplay work {#script-and-canon-revision-from-screenplay-work}

Final screenplay is the last authoring layer that can expose an inherited defect before source realization. Report the exact defect it reveals: an emotional consequence nothing upstream earned, access or information order that cannot survive dramatization, progression that reads as arbitrary when seen and heard, a settings fact that becomes visibly false, or a subject drive unable to carry the final action or line. Repair the earliest owning script, treatment, or settings unit and propagate downward; audiovisual polish may not compensate for an unchosen parent decision.

`@evidence` names every upstream target repaired and the visible, audible, rhythmic, or dramatic realization that exposed it. When this unit exposes no defect, `@evidenceExclude` names its actual script parent and the concrete consequence, access, progression, fact, and drive realized without alteration. A silent pass, parentless unit, or repeated sufficiency sentence covers nothing.

Review question: what did realizing this screenplay unit prove wrong in its actual script, treatment, or settings parents, or which concrete parent decisions did it test and find sufficient?

Sources: [Detailed Outline Control on controlled refinement from outline to final text](https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.190/); [George Mason University on revising content and organization before sentence form](https://writingcenter.gmu.edu/writing-resources/writing-as-process/revising)
