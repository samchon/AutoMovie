# Treatment upstream revision

What writing a treatment proves wrong in settings, and what is repaired there. Every selected treatment H2 answers for the settings facts it actually uses.

## Settings revision from treatment work {#settings-revision-from-treatment-work}

Treatment is the first practical test of canon. Report the exact parent defect it exposes: a fact too loose to use, incompatible constraints, a subject whose established drives cannot produce the needed turn, a participant with no settings owner, or an unresolved value without which progression cannot be chosen. Repair the settings H2 that owns it and inherit the repair; a treatment-local alternative, refusal, capability, or limit is not an upstream correction.

`@evidence` names every settings target repaired and what authoring this unit exposed. When this unit exposes no defect, `@evidenceExclude` names the concrete settings facts, constraints, subjects, and drives tested and why they were sufficient for this progression. A silent pass, parentless unit, "no change needed", or sentence repeated across units covers nothing.

Review question: what did authoring this treatment unit prove wrong in its actual settings parents, or which concrete parent decisions did it test and find sufficient?

Sources: [Detailed Outline Control on moving creative decisions upstream](https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.190/); [NASA on distinguishing symptom removal from root-cause correction](https://swehb.nasa.gov/spaces/SWEHBVC/pages/50889445/SWE-204%2B-%2BProcess%2BAssessments)
