# Capture

Capture is an observation of an exact source result under declared conditions. The scaffold supplies a live source viewer, not a stored capture or delivery service. Implement a requested capture through the public viewer and render APIs in production-owned source; do not recreate a project state store.

## Declare the observation

Before capturing, name the source revision and explicit inputs, subject or shot, camera, time or pose, pass, raster, frame clock, and actual browser and graphics runtime. Reuse the producer inspected by the source viewer. A stale result, substituted camera, smaller raster, or different runtime is a different observation.

Inspect the actual graphics vendor and renderer before claiming hardware acceleration. A requested backend is not evidence of the backend that drew the picture. Keep the runtime and presentation conditions fixed for comparisons.

## Select the complete view set

Derive views from the contract being judged before drawing them. A shot owes its declared frame-and-pass population. An isolated model needs the required silhouette, opposing perspectives, relevant articulation extremes, and difficult or occluded details. Name which complete population a sample represents and the contract that permits that inference.

Use beauty for appearance and the applicable structural passes for geometry, identity, depth, normals, outlines, and pose. A structural pass cannot establish lighting or material appearance. A sectioned or isolated subject cannot discharge an audience-frame requirement.

For semantic masks, derive the palette and subject identities from the same typed result as the image. Colors without their exact semantic mapping prove no identity. Inspect unmapped and omitted populations rather than accepting a plausible color image.

## Inspect the actual output

Open each image and verify it decodes, has the declared dimensions, and depicts the requested subject at the requested camera, time, and state. Play the interval when the claim concerns motion or synchronization. A successful API return, output path, console screenshot, or image copied from another revision is not an observed result.

Record the source basis, actual runtime, output identity, complete planned population, and concrete observation in the authored review. Preserve refused, missing, partial, stale, and unsupported outcomes. A picture that changed after observation must be inspected again.

## Correct failures

Separate invalid source, invalid request, unavailable host, changed input, undecodable media, and incomplete observation. Correct the named owner and repeat the affected population on one stable basis. Do not retry invalid input unchanged, silently clamp authored conditions, or report surviving pictures as a complete sweep.

[Production review](review.md) owns the judgment and its consequences. [Inspection](inspection.md) owns structural queries that need no rendered image.
