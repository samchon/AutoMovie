# Treatments

Treatments belong only to a film. Write detailed narrative treatments in `docs/treatments` with ordered filenames and explicitly anchored `H2 sequence -> H3 scene -> H4 beat` identities.

## Scope

A treatment is a **detailed narrative treatment**. It is not a causal card, not a lore dump, not an event ledger, and not a preliminary screenplay. A careful reader holding only the settings and the treatment must be able to state what the film does and why it matters, before a script or a shot exists.

An H4 establishes its inherited condition, participants or active forces, material and social constraints, central event or formal operation, available choice or resistance where applicable, changed state, audience change, and bridge to what follows. Write enough particularity to make the development testable: who is present, who knows what, what is physically or institutionally possible, and how the chosen action changes what can follow. A recurring motif or returning subject acquires changed context or effect through use rather than reappearing as decoration.

Ground emotion in cause, decision, expression, or changed relationship. A label such as "trust deepens" reports a result the treatment never developed. Do not pad a unit with scenery, research, or world detail that changes no action, perception, power, cost, or formal development.

The treatment may quote a short line when its exact wording, lie, refusal, or silence is the hinge of the beat, but line-by-line dialogue, blocking, exact timing, camera grammar, and implementation belong downstream. A treatment is more detailed than an outline because it says what happens and why it matters; a script is more detailed because it says exactly how the event unfolds through space, time, process, speech, and response.

Apply `docs/obligations/treatments.md#treatment-boundary` by handing the settings and complete treatment to a cold reader and asking for the matching script. Any essential narrative decision that reader cannot recover belongs in the earliest treatment unit rather than downstream.

## Unit map and granularity

Before prose, rerun the [production-specific contract](work-specific.md) pass with the `discovery/common.md`, `discovery/films.md`, and `discovery/treatments.md` targets, and implement each retained result in its target and claim before relying on it.

Then make a unit map with every planned H2/H3/H4 key and slug, entry, central progression, exit, audience change, and parent relation. Apply the narrative unit-addressability obligation across the complete H2 owner population before accepting it. Every beat becomes a script and screenplay beat, so an unworkable count or unit size is repaired here instead of being hidden later by summary.

After drafting, audit every umbrella and beat for a hidden independent progression. Split overloaded units, merge fragments without independent function, or revise the planned scale under its owning authority. Reverse-outline direct paragraphs under the treatment-paragraph principle.

A count the later layers cannot realize is never solved by thinning units into reports of their own events. Redesign the granularity: split what is overloaded, join what has no independent function, or change the planned unit size while preserving the declared scale. Changing the declared scale itself needs explicit user approval and a settings revision first.

## Gate

Start at `treatments: "draft"` only after settings are in `review`. Before `evidence`, read the complete treatment as an audience member and test every unit's arrival, development, departure, long-range consequence, information state, applicable agency, legibility of deliberate jumps, and proportional realization of the declared film scale. Compare the hierarchy to the unit map and reject placeholders, event lists, causal cards, inert lore, false bridges, and hidden units.

Run [Author process Self-Review](self-review.md) to its clean round before every stage transition and again after any repair.

Every H2, H3, and H4 answers the complete common, narrative, and treatment principle checklists and cites only settings it actually uses. The H2 population supplies every common, narrative, and treatment obligation owner, including `obligations/treatments.md#sustained-middle`, and covers its applicable discovery targets. Follow [Evidence staging](evidence-staging.md) for exact claim behavior. A later narrative change begins in the smallest true treatment owner and propagates through script, screenplay, shot, and film source.
