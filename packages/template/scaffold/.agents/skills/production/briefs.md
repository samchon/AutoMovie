# Direct briefs

Briefs belong only to a `brief` production. Use `docs/briefs` for one bounded audiovisual question whose complete intent fits one delivery/shot/observation hierarchy. A simple short-form action may qualify; a result that needs independent dramatic causality, character-choice, or disclosure refinement does not. Runtime does not decide eligibility.

Use explicitly anchored `H2 delivery -> H3 shot -> H4 observation` units. The H2 states the complete question and initial-to-terminal progression, each H3 allocates one shot's contribution, and each H4 defines the viewing condition and result that can answer or falsify it. Cite settings and only the active map, model, space, material, instance, motion, and system branches actually consumed.

Before drafting, make an address map for the delivery, every shot, and every observation. Apply `obligations/delivery/briefs.md#brief-unit-addressability` across the complete H2 owner population: expose a hidden independent delivery so single-scope eligibility can reject or migrate it, split a hidden second shot or separately falsifiable observation, and merge a fragment that cannot move, change, be cited, or be judged independently. After drafting, reverse-outline every H2, H3, and H4 under `principles/delivery/briefs.md#brief-information-structure` so its owner, conditions, observation, falsifying boundary, and any descendant results can be found without reconstructing them from scattered prose.

If those meanings require decisions separate from the bounded shot observations, stop and migrate the production to `film`. Do not smuggle that authorship into source code or compress a film ladder into one brief.

Briefs do not select `obligations/story/narratives.md` or `principles/story/narratives.md`. Their addressability and information structure are governed by the brief-specific targets above in delivery, shot, and observation terms; importing film-only audience investment, character continuity, or vertical narrative inheritance would contradict the brief boundary.

## Gate

Start at `briefs: "draft"` after settings review. Design branches may proceed in parallel; each newly active reviewed branch adds its foundation targets and reopens affected brief evidence. Before `evidence`, verify that one document completely allocates the observable contract, the address map contains no hidden or artificial unit boundary, every H2, H3, and H4 answers the common and brief principle checklists, the H2 population supplies every common and brief-obligation owner, every observation is falsifiable at its declared condition, and no downstream shot must invent narrative or design meaning. Shots still wait for the reviewed source corresponding to every active design branch.

Run [Author process Self-Review](self-review.md) to its clean round before the transition. Follow [Evidence staging](evidence-staging.md).
