Each shift in diction, syntax, address, dialect, or register in eligible dialogue, narration, or audience-read text belongs to a speaker, narrator, document form, audience, period, relationship, or production decision. A register shift imported only to sound polished, dramatic, or generically cinematic has no owner. Preserve the identity and speech act fixed by construction while repairing only its English realization. Technical register in a mechanical description remains exact construction text rather than material for smoothing.

Review question: who or what owns each departure from the unit's established English register, and which departure changes expression without changing its intended meaning?
