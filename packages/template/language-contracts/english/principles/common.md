# English principles

## Idiomatic relation {#english-idiomatic-relation}

Each unit uses the declared English variety to express the exact relation among actions, objects, speakers, and claims. Abstract nouns, copulas, contrasts, lists, and marked punctuation remain valid when their literal function is present; replacing concrete relations with a generic elevated frame fails.

Review question: which sentence requires the reader to reconstruct a concrete relation hidden by an abstract or generic English frame?

## Register ownership {#english-register-ownership}

Each shift in diction, syntax, address, dialect, or technical register belongs to a speaker, document form, audience, period, or production decision. A register shift imported only to sound polished or dramatic has no owner.

Review question: who or what owns each departure from the unit's established English register?
