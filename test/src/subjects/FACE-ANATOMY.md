# Anatomical form and control inventory

This is the direct portrait's anatomical design inventory. The [inspection record](generated-korean-girl-01/review.md) identifies the current captured source and observation coverage. The selected construction is the procedural measured nose and support field; the CC0 nasal-patch alternative was rejected for visible joining defects. The first subject's fit was frozen after 57 minutes in issue #2469 and remains unaccepted. Shared construction has moved to `@automovie/human`, including document-level frame, cranial/cervical, side-specific pinna and expression controls. The tables distinguish that implemented control surface from unresolved form. This inventory is not portrait acceptance or whole-PR Self-Review completion. The [construction design](FACE-DESIGN.md) and [public face specification](../../../docs/specifications/asset-and-representation/facial-authoring/contract.md) own their respective construction and document contracts.

An anatomical name can denote an underlying support, an exposed surface, a section, a boundary or an attachment. It does not require a separate visible mesh. A generic displacement helper can move a region, but it does not establish that the region has an adequate anatomical construction. The landmark cage supplies image correspondences and inferred depth; it is not an accepted three-dimensional smile or anatomical baseline.

The status descriptions below distinguish an absent form owner, an existing but insufficient representation, fixed internal constants, controls outside the active recipe, and a visible defect despite a working parameter. Proposed controls are design work, not implemented TypeScript exports. No row is completed by naming its future interface.

## Detailed parameter and attachment contract {#detail-contract}

Each incomplete owner above needs an optional detailed representation appropriate to its form. A longitudinal list of sections, a boundary/control-point array or a nested group object is valid. Its input must state the owner, local frame, units, section ordering and whether it replaces the base or adds a small residual. `undefined` retains the complete basic formula; it does not certify that the basic formula matches this portrait.

At minimum, a detailed section distinguishes its placement from its shape: longitudinal position, transverse span, anterior/posterior profile, crest/crease positions, endpoint tangents and the interfaces it shares. Where a scalar cannot express a varying contour, use ordered samples or sections. Independently authored right/left values must reach the actual assembly, including ears and orbital support, rather than stopping at an unused helper signature.

The eye group owns aperture/canthi, optical frame, lid margin, tissue sections and the surrounding support join. The midface group coordinates lower orbit, cheek, nasal sidewall and commissure. The nasal group coordinates tip/ala/columella/sill/aperture/vestibule. The upper oral region coordinates subnasale, philtral curves, Cupid peaks, skin roll and vermilion. Shared positions and derivatives are computed once, while physically separate surfaces retain the appropriate contact relationship.

Internal bones, muscles, retaining attachments and fat compartments supply responsibilities and derivation when they affect visible form or movement. They need not all become separate rendered objects. Conversely, the absence of a separate mesh cannot excuse a missing visible curvature, boundary, volume or motion relationship.

## Consequence and verification priorities {#verification-priorities}

At the recorded baseline GLB `454cd902`, an owner-omission measurement rebuilt every head/attached part and first required exact equality with captured model `177c63a8`. Keeping the same refined connectivity, omitting both cheek layers changed final skin by up to 6.458 mm; omitting only the philtral fields changed it by 0.235 mm; omitting orbital support changed it by 1.396 mm; disabling corneal contact changed it by 2.328 mm. Thus these owners do affect the final geometry. These maxima occur at different vertices, measure displacement rather than tissue volume, and do not rank likeness or establish the cause of every visible defect. The unchanged-baseline check, frozen artifact identities, script hash and exact observations are retained in the local anatomical-owner measurement receipt and PR chronology.

The upper orbit, lower-lid/cheek system, nasal base and upper-lip/perioral system all have identified design debt. None should be rediscovered as a surprise after polishing another region. Resolve their representation and shared boundaries before choosing the next coherent implementation slice. The implementation boundary may include several related parts.

For each slice, compare the retained base and the detailed replacement at the final geometry and render. Measure the actual contour, section, placement and affected neighbours, including whether subdivision or contact attenuates/overwrites an authored value. Inspect colour and clay, close and assembled views. Check omitted/default equivalence, independent sides, input ownership, topology/contact, parameter sensitivity and the declared unchanged population. A successful formula test, more exported fields or a smaller fitting residual does not establish the photographed form.

This inventory identifies expressive responsibilities; it does not establish a mathematical guarantee of likeness from one photograph. Acceptance requires the resulting geometry and frames to satisfy the source observations, including connected form between named parts. Extraction preserves the frozen direct-study result through the actual package consumer path; that result is explicitly not an accepted likeness.
