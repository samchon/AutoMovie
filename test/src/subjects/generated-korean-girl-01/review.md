This is an inspected, unfinished face. **The likeness is not accepted.** Hair is a coarse silhouette mass; hair and eyebrow detail and the torso remain deferred. The latest complete model, comparison and captures live in `.shots/face-experiment/preview/`. Publication replaces that directory only after GLTF export, all fourteen frames and their hash checks succeed.

The reviewed GLB is `portrait.glb`, SHA-256 `4f1721b36cb6c866829b7cdc51fe00ddd70b68e6a0bec50edbc153efc92d4951`. Profile SHA-256 is `56cb3bed90492a7ce35066843aa15fd78c9375c1d152f4e5ee856ab629c8d777`. Blender 5.1.2 imported this actual GLB and rendered it with Cycles on CPU at 256 samples without denoising. The source comparison, nine-view contact sheet, three clay views and independent calibration cube were opened and inspected. The cube has a visible cast shadow to its lower right. Clay hides the coarse hair to expose the head. Source review companions are not generated by the renderer.

## Component replacement {#component-replacement}

The component tests exercise independent eye/nose/mouth replacement, adaptation of surrounding skin and the refined opening used by interior parts. Dental tests place unequal crowns on a hand-defined 45-degree guide: zero authored gap gives zero guide clearance, and a 0.2 mm gap changes centre spacing without shrinking crown width. Cheek tests check copied settings, current-host attachments, millimetre-to-metre conversion, neutral relief and groove-depth stability with changed width or unevenly spaced anchors. Brow tests check normal clearance on a sloping plane, translated skin, copied profiles, disabled fibres and invalid or unsupported paths. These are numerical attachment checks.

The captured GLB's 45,360 brow vertices and 67,200 brow triangle centres were compared with the front envelope of its actual skin. No sampled point lay below the skin; the minimum sampled Z clearance was approximately 0.025 mm. This is a sampled contact check, not an exact triangle-intersection proof. Denoising is disabled in this capture; some sampling noise remains in the corneal reflections and neck shadows.

Alternate component assemblies have not been captured and visually reviewed against this current source revision. Their numerical tests do not substitute for the pending replacement renders.

Nostril sizing is verified independently in a hand-defined plane `y=z`: doubling its height doubles Y and Z together, retains X, and places the lining's support ring from that changed boundary. Profile-plane sizing, nonplanarity, translation, unit changes, copied identity and numerical refusals have separate assertions. The current fit scales aperture height to 0.65 while retaining the measured rim and applying no additional tilt. Smaller apertures do not establish correct alar or columellar anatomy.
