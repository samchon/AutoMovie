# Settings principles

Settings state the production's facts, constraints, identities, and capabilities before any representation, motion, narrative order, or shot is selected.

## Fact status {#fact-status}

Every settings unit distinguishes an externally supported fact, a production invention, an inherited default, a derived value, a contested matter, and an unresolved decision, and states the scope in which that status is valid. Inference, dispute, and provisional choice do not masquerade as settled external fact.

A contested matter identifies the competing claims and the authority or production decision that governs while the dispute remains. An unresolved fact states what would settle it. A downstream layer may not depend on an unresolved answer until it is researched or adopted here.

Review question: what supported, invented, defaulted, derived, contested, or unresolved status, authority, derivation, and scope make each stated fact usable downstream?

This item classifies the production fact. `common.md#declared-basis` traces a document statement to the basis on which that document may make it, and `source-support` judges external support.

Sources: [W3C PROV-O](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/); [NIST on metrological traceability](https://www.nist.gov/calibrations/traceability)

## Source support {#source-support}

Every externally checkable historical, legal, scientific, medical, cultural, or technical precision points to direct evidence that actually supports it. Prefer primary material, official records, standards, scholarship, and critical editions; record material date, scope, bias, conflict, and uncertainty. Search results, collection portals, broad home pages, and remembered facts are discovery routes, not support for a narrower claim.

Name the document, record item, edition, section, page, date, or direct page needed to retrieve the evidence. When direct support cannot justify the precision, mark the fact unresolved or reduce the precision. Do not attach external authority to a pure production invention.

Review question: can another author retrieve the exact supporting passage and see that its scope and uncertainty match the settings claim?

This item judges the support for a settings fact. Research principles own the optional ledger's source record and production consequence; fact status owns the label attached to the resulting production fact.

Sources: [NARA on retrievable record citation](https://www.archives.gov/legislative/research/citation.html); [W3C PROV-O on primary sources and derivation](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/); [NIST Technical Note 1297 on stating uncertainty](https://www.nist.gov/pml/nist-technical-note-1297)

## Capability boundary {#capability-boundary}

Every subject or environment capability states the semantic state it may change, its limits, costs, dependencies, exceptions, relevant inability, and the frame and units in which those limits are expressed. Settings authorize what can happen; they do not choose when a scene uses it, which representation interface exposes it, or which timed path realizes it.

Review question: could a downstream author decide whether an intended action is permitted without inventing an interface, motion, or scene?

This item owns what an addressed subject or environment may change in the production world. `obligations/core/settings.md#design-dependent-subject-conditions` owns the user, equipment, load, assistance, uncertainty, and other conditions the delivery must accommodate or measure rather than the subject's own capability. Model articulation owns the stable representation interface, and motion parameter domain owns the allowed inputs and degrees of freedom used over time.

Sources: [glTF 2.0 coordinate-system and units requirements](https://registry.khronos.org/glTF/specs/2.0/glTF-2.0.html#coordinate-system-and-units); [OpenUSD model and prim concepts](https://openusd.org/release/glossary.html)

## Constraint sufficiency {#constraint-sufficiency}

Every adopted rule or constraint supplies enough boundary, operating condition, authority, resource, dependency, cost, limit, and exception for lower layers to judge a choice without inventing a production fact. When one value cannot yet be fixed, state an admissible range or the condition that decides it.

Review question: which plausible downstream choice would still require guessing because this rule lacks a limit, exception, or decision condition?

This item owns the decision boundary of an adopted constraint. The common substantive-completion principle asks whether the complete H2 is a usable settings deliverable, and the settings minimal-departure obligation decides which inherited defaults need no restatement.

Sources: [Ronen on necessary indeterminacy in fictional worlds](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/possible-worlds-in-literary-theory/fictional-entities-incomplete-beings/EA5E36B2C5D64AE648FD1D993E6F92CC); [NASA systems engineering handbook on bounded requirements and interfaces](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/systems-engineering-handbook/)

## Observable identity {#observable-identity}

Every settings unit that defines a subject, place, background, or audible identity describes its observable form in enough concrete detail that independent downstream authors imagine and preserve the same owner under the population's delivery review condition. For a person or creature, settle the applicable body plan, proportions, scale, silhouette, face and head structure, hair or covering, posture, habitual expression, skin or surface appearance, distinguishing marks, clothing or carried identity, color relations, visible age or wear, and allowed state variation. For a place or background, settle the applicable spatial character, massing, horizon or enclosure, dominant shapes, landmark relationships, scale cues, circulation or sightline identity, surface and color fields, weathering, atmosphere, time-dependent appearance, and allowed variation. Use production-specific observations rather than aesthetic labels, stock archetypes, or a list copied from this paragraph.

Detail is proportional to downstream consequence but never collapses to a name, genre, mood, or single flattering view. Describe the invariant identity and the variations a scene may show from the neutral or reference state and every materially different useful angle. Whenever reproduction depends on precision, state unit-bearing dimensions or ranges, proportions, landmark and component relations in the production coordinate convention, surface-zone boundaries, articulation or change limits, and front, profile, rear, overhead, or underside evidence as applicable. Aesthetic prose and a reference image without these relations do not settle observable identity.

This mechanical precision is canon, not a request for literary naturalness. This owner states what must be recognizable and how its parts relate; map, model, space, material, instance, motion, camera, lighting, and sound owners choose the deterministic implementation within those facts.

Review question: could two independent design authors reconstruct observably different subjects or places while both claiming to follow this description, and which missing form, relation, surface, scale, or variation decision permits that divergence?

Sources: [Academy Digital Source Master on defined image characteristics](https://www.oscars.org/science-technology/sci-tech-projects/academy-digital-source-master); [glTF 2.0 scene and asset concepts](https://registry.khronos.org/glTF/specs/2.0/glTF-2.0.html#concepts)
