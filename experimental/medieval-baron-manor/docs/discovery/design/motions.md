# Motion Discovery

The motion-specific outcome that the complete active motion H2 population must search for and account for. Apply `common.md` and `designs.md` in the same pass for broader production contracts.

## Work-specific motion requirements {#work-specific-motion-requirements}

Discover every production-specific transition condition required by the actual changing subjects and their reviewed interfaces. Contact, entry and exit state, timing and phase, path and spatial relation, invariants and tolerances, parameter domain, composition, interruption, handoff, and neutral observation are possible search directions rather than a checklist. Derive the requirements from the authorized setting capability, consumed map, model, space, material, instance, or system interface, and planned use rather than a generic animation recipe, and give each material result an actual motion H2 owner or a production-local principle, obligation, or evidence relationship with a typed claim.

A positive acknowledgement identifies the current authored owners and contract wiring. If the complete search finds no independent motion result, one population-wide exclusion names the concrete contact, endpoint, phase, path, invariant, parameter, composition, interruption, and observation risks examined and the shared owners that fully cover them. An animation name, duration, easing curve, or motion style without its governed states, interfaces, and observable transition does not cover this duty.

Review question: which transition condition is specific to these subjects, contacts, or compositions and absent from the reusable motion contract?

Sources: [glTF 2.0 on animation samplers, channels, targets, and interpolation](https://registry.khronos.org/glTF/specs/2.0/glTF-2.0.html#animations); [W3C Web Animations on timing, effect association, and composition](https://www.w3.org/TR/web-animations-1/)
