# Space Discovery

The space-specific outcome that the complete active space H2 population must search for and account for. Apply `common.md` and `designs.md` in the same pass for broader production contracts.

## Work-specific space requirements {#work-specific-space-requirements}

Discover every production-specific spatial condition required by the actual world, site, building, exterior, interior, room, zone, opening, and route scope. Effective dimensions, circulation and egress, accessibility, openings, storey relationships, structure or service interfaces, site constraints, and applicable code or standard consequences are possible search directions rather than a checklist. Derive the requirements from the declared occupancy and use, setting, jurisdiction or adopted standard, source records, users, operators, and planned observations rather than a generic building program. Keep source authority and production-wide applicability in research or settings, and give each material spatial result an actual space H2 owner or a production-local principle, obligation, or evidence relationship with a typed claim.

A positive acknowledgement identifies the current authored owners, decision basis, and contract wiring. If the complete search finds no independent space result, one population-wide exclusion names the concrete site, dimension, route, opening, level, envelope, access, standard, and observation risks examined and the shared owners that fully cover them. A building type, room list, nominal dimension, code label, or assertion of accessibility without an applicable source, effective spatial consequence, and falsifying observation does not cover this duty.

Review question: which spatial constraint is specific to this site, building, use, or adopted standard and absent from the reusable space contract?

Sources: [buildingSMART IFC 4.3 on explicit space-boundary relationships](https://standards.buildingsmart.org/IFC/RELEASE/IFC4_3/HTML/lexical/IfcRelSpaceBoundary.htm); [U.S. Department of Justice 2010 ADA Standards on accessible routes, clearances, openings, and building elements](https://www.ada.gov/law-and-regs/design-standards/2010-stds/)
