# Motion upstream revision

What exercising a deterministic transition proves wrong in settings or consumed design units, and what is repaired there. Every selected motion H2 answers for its actual parents.

## Parent revision from motion work {#parent-revision-from-motion-work}

Motion work tests whether declared capabilities and writable interfaces survive actual inputs, time, contact, and terminal states. Report the exact parent defect it exposes: a missing joint or state channel, an unreachable pose, contradictory limits, a route or collision boundary that blocks the transition, a system dependency with no time basis, or a capability that settings cannot authorize. Repair the earliest settings or design owner; do not add a private degree of freedom or convenient discontinuity in motion.

`@evidence` names every upstream target repaired and the transition sample that exposed it. When this unit exposes no defect, `@evidenceExclude` names the concrete interfaces, limits, contacts, dependencies, and boundary states exercised and why they were sufficient. A parentless unit or generic playback claim covers nothing.

Review question: what did exercising this motion unit prove wrong in its actual settings or design parents, or which concrete parent decisions did it test and find sufficient?

Sources: [Web Animations on explicit timing inputs and sampled effect state](https://www.w3.org/TR/web-animations-1/); [NASA on verification findings and root-cause correction](https://swehb.nasa.gov/spaces/SWEHBVC/pages/50889445/SWE-204%2B-%2BProcess%2BAssessments)
