# Screenplay naturalness principles

Film-specific expression questions for final screenplay units. Construction owns what the scene means and requires; this family owns how the frozen meaning reads and performs for an audience and an independent shot author.

## Scene pressure shapes expression {#naturalness-scene-pressure}

Action lines, dialogue blocks, captions, sounds, silences, and transitions take their length, syntax, density, and cadence from the current physical and dramatic pressure. Unlike scenes do not converge on one explanatory frame or interchangeable block rhythm merely because each sentence is locally grammatical. A refrain, ritual, procedural form, montage, or declared formal system remains when the production owns the recurrence.

Revise only the presentation of already authored actions, relations, and audiovisual facts. A missing escalation, response, consequence, cue, or timing boundary is a construction defect and cannot be fabricated to improve flow.

Review question: where does this unit's expression respond to its changing pressure, and which passage keeps an imported cadence after the scene has moved on?

Sources: [Zwaan, Langston, and Graesser on situation changes](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.1995.tb00513.x); [Purdue OWL on sentence-pattern variation](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/academic_writing/sentence_variety/similar_sentence_patterns_or_rhythms.html)

Read each exchange and designed sound in sequence with the action around it and identify what changes hands: information, leverage, obligation, refusal, attention, mood, bodily response, or audience interpretation. A line or cue fails when it merely restates an adjacent block, announces the scene's meaning as a portable proposition, or shares one interchangeable figure with every source while producing no distinct effect. Repetition that is itself the action, ritual response, command, confirmation, refrain, and deliberate acoustic motif remain valid when their consequence is legible.

Reword the expression to reveal the inherited tactic, relation, source identity, or effect. Preserve what every speaker knows, seeks, says, withholds, gains, and loses and what every sound denotes. If the exchange or cue lacks the substantive content needed to act, return it to scripts or screenplay construction.

Review question: what changes for the hearer, source, scene, or audience after each consequential line, silence, and sound, and which one merely duplicates what is already present?

Sources: [Cambridge Companion to Narrative on dialogue as action](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/cambridge-companion-to-narrative/dialogue/DA260BBCE7DBBA9BCB4196CE7F35ABDC); [Center for Fiction on dialogue function and subtext](https://centerforfiction.org/writing-tools/tips-for-writing-dialogue/); [Chion on sound and image](https://cup.columbia.edu/book/audio-vision-sound-on-screen/9780231185882/)
