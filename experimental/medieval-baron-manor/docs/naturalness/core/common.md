# Common naturalness principles

Language-neutral expression questions that every final screenplay H2, H3, and H4 answers for its dialogue, narration, and audience-read language after the complete construction screenplay is frozen. These questions never authorize a new event, fact, motive, action, speech act, timing decision, audiovisual requirement, physical description, or unit identity. A missing or wrong substantive decision returns to its earliest construction owner before revision resumes.

Judge eligible language in the whole unit's context, not by isolated surface markers. Mechanically exact descriptions of external appearance, geometry, material, spatial relation, physical state, contact, motion, or timing remain construction text and are not naturalized. Population recurrence belongs to `obligations/core/defaults.md`, target-language grammar and register belong to the installed language naturalness module, and screenplay-specific performance belongs to `naturalness/story/screenplays.md`.

Take what eligible dialogue, narration, or audience-read language adds beyond its bare statement, including a list member, modifier, qualification, formatting mark, contrast, transition, or restatement, and ask what the audience loses when each goes. An element whose removal costs no information, perspective, pressure, rhythm, characterization, or formal effect fails while it stands. The count or form is never the finding: repetition, parallelism, fragmentation, marked punctuation, silence, and deliberate flatness remain valid when their function is legible.

Clarify the distinction, action, boundary, or effect already present in eligible language, or remove language that contributes nothing. Do not edit a mechanical audiovisual clause under this rule. If the repair requires a new fact, action, exchange, physical or audiovisual cue, or timing decision, stop and return the defect to construction.

Review question: what does every expressive element make the audience perceive or carry forward, and which element can disappear without changing that experience?

Sources: [Purdue OWL on sentence variety and functional patterns](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/academic_writing/sentence_variety/index.html); [Harvard Writing Center on transitions](https://writingcenter.fas.harvard.edu/transitions)

## Reads as performed, not assembled {#naturalness-performed-whole}


After other naturalness findings are repaired, read the complete unit once with its unchanged mechanical descriptions and state how its eligible language performs around them. Dialogue, narration, or audience-read language that passes sentence-level checks yet still feels assembled from individually acceptable fragments fails; revise that eligible expression as a whole while preserving every construction invariant. An answer that merely reports compliance, repeats other checklist answers, or infers authorship from a surface marker has not performed this reading.

Spare, plain, severe, stylized, documentary, and deliberately mechanical audience language can pass on the production's declared terms. Mechanically exact external description is required to remain mechanical even when it would sound unnatural as prose. This item asks whether eligible language coheres with the unit's action and intended audience experience, not whether the whole screenplay resembles a generic notion of literary human writing.

Review question: after every local finding is closed, how do this unit's parts perform together and what exact audience experience remains?

Sources: [Bordwell on films as patterned systems](https://www.routledge.com/Narration-in-the-Fiction-Film/Bordwell/p/book/9780415018777); [Chion on audiovisual perception](https://cup.columbia.edu/book/audio-vision-sound-on-screen/9780231185882/)
