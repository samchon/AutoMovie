# Common naturalness principles

Language-neutral expression questions that every final screenplay H2, H3, and H4 answers after the complete construction screenplay is frozen. These questions never authorize a new event, fact, motive, action, speech act, timing decision, audiovisual requirement, or unit identity. A missing or wrong substantive decision returns to its earliest construction owner before revision resumes.

Judge the whole unit in context, not isolated surface markers. Population recurrence belongs to `obligations/core/defaults.md`, target-language grammar and register belong to the installed language naturalness module, and screenplay-specific performance belongs to `naturalness/story/screenplays.md`.

Take what the unit adds beyond its bare action or statement, including a list member, modifier, qualification, formatting mark, contrast, transition, or restatement, and ask what the audience loses when each goes. An element whose removal costs no information, perspective, pressure, rhythm, characterization, sound, image, or formal effect fails while it stands. The count or form is never the finding: repetition, parallelism, fragmentation, marked punctuation, silence, and deliberate flatness remain valid when their function is legible.

Clarify the distinction, action, boundary, or effect already present in the construction screenplay, or remove expression that contributes nothing. If the repair requires a new fact, action, exchange, audiovisual cue, or timing decision, stop and return the defect to construction.

Review question: what does every expressive element make the audience perceive or carry forward, and which element can disappear without changing that experience?

Sources: [Purdue OWL on sentence variety and functional patterns](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/academic_writing/sentence_variety/index.html); [Harvard Writing Center on transitions](https://writingcenter.fas.harvard.edu/transitions)

## Reads as performed, not assembled {#naturalness-performed-whole}


After other naturalness findings are repaired, read the complete unit once as a performed audiovisual passage and state what it leaves. A unit that passes sentence-level checks yet still feels assembled from individually acceptable blocks fails; revise the expression as a whole while preserving every construction invariant. An answer that merely reports compliance, repeats other checklist answers, or infers authorship from a surface marker has not performed this reading.

Spare, plain, severe, stylized, documentary, and deliberately mechanical writing can pass on the production's declared terms. This item asks whether the unit's action, speech, sound, silence, text, and transition cohere into its intended audience experience, not whether it resembles a generic notion of human writing.

Review question: after every local finding is closed, how do this unit's parts perform together and what exact audience experience remains?

Sources: [Bordwell on films as patterned systems](https://www.routledge.com/Narration-in-the-Fiction-Film/Bordwell/p/book/9780415018777); [Chion on audiovisual perception](https://cup.columbia.edu/book/audio-vision-sound-on-screen/9780231185882/)
